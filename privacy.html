RunCity — Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 August 2026

RunCity does not have accounts, does not track you, and does not send your runs anywhere. Almost everything below is a description of data that never leaves your phone.

What Stays on Your Device

Your runs — distance, duration, pace, elevation, route track, completed landmarks and any selfies you take — are stored only in RunCity's own local database on your iPhone. There is no RunCity account, no RunCity server, and no upload. Selfies are kept inside the app's private storage and are not written to your photo library unless you save or share them yourself.

Your profile details (name, home city, weight, unit preferences) are also stored only on your device. Weight is used solely to estimate calories.

Location

RunCity needs your location to draw your position on the route, to trigger audio narration when you reach a landmark, and to record the run you just did. Location is used while the app is in the background so narration keeps working with your screen locked.

RunCity itself does not transmit your location. See "Maps" below for the one exception, which comes from our map provider rather than from us.

Apple Health

If you allow it, RunCity saves each finished run to Apple Health as a workout (including its route), so it appears in Health and in other apps you have connected such as Strava. If you allow it, RunCity also reads your average heart rate for the period of the run to show on your run summary.

This exchange is entirely between RunCity and Apple Health on your device. Health data is never sent to us. You can revoke either permission at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health, and RunCity will keep working without it.

Camera and Motion

The camera is used only when you choose to take a selfie at a landmark. Motion data (step cadence) is used only to smooth the pace shown on screen where GPS accuracy is poor, such as narrow streets. Neither is transmitted.

Maps

RunCity displays maps using the Mapbox Maps SDK. To draw maps and to download the offline map for a city, the SDK contacts Mapbox's servers, and it also sends Mapbox its own usage and location telemetry for analytics. That data is handled by Mapbox under Mapbox's privacy policy, not ours.

You can turn Mapbox's telemetry off: tap the (i) button on any map in RunCity and choose the telemetry setting. Maps continue to work with it disabled.

What We Do Not Do

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind. We do not build a profile of you. We do not sell, rent or share your data, because we do not receive it. RunCity does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework because it does not track you across apps or websites.

Deleting Your Data

Delete an individual run from the My Runs tab; its selfies are deleted with it. Downloaded offline maps can be removed separately under Settings › Downloaded data, and will download again the next time you open that city's map. To remove everything at once, delete the app — because all of your RunCity data lives on your device, uninstalling removes it. Workouts you previously sent to Apple Health stay in Health, and are deleted from there.

Children

RunCity is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes
