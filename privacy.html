CityRun — Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

CityRun does not have accounts, does not track you, and never sends your runs to us. Everything below describes data that stays on your phone, or in your own iCloud, unless you choose to share it — apart from anonymous usage counts, described under USAGE DATA, which you can switch off.

What Stays on Your Device

Your runs — distance, duration, pace, elevation, route track, completed landmarks and any selfies you take — are stored in CityRun's own database on your iPhone. There is no CityRun account, and nothing is uploaded to us. Selfies are kept inside the app's private storage and are not written to your photo library unless you save or share them yourself.

Your profile details (name, username, bio, age, home city, weight, unit preferences) are stored the same way. Weight is used solely to estimate calories.

Crash reports are kept on your phone and leave it only if you share them from Settings › Crash reports. This week's totals are shared with CityRun's home-screen widget, on the same phone.

Icloud Backup

If you are signed in to iCloud, CityRun keeps a copy of your runs, selfies, profile and settings in your private iCloud storage, so they survive a lost or replaced phone and appear on your other devices. That copy lives in your Apple account; Apple stores it, and we cannot see it. Turn it off in CityRun's Settings › iCloud. To delete what is already there: iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › CityRun.

Location

CityRun needs your location to draw your position on the route, to trigger audio narration when you reach a landmark, to give turn directions, and to record the run you just did. During a run, location keeps being used while the app is in the background so narration and directions keep working with your screen locked.

While the run clubs list is open, CityRun also uses your location to put the nearest clubs first.

When Explore opens, or when you tap Show where you are in the city list, CityRun checks your location once, on your phone, to find the nearest CityRun city. It is not kept or sent anywhere.

CityRun does not transmit your location, and neither does anything built into it.

Apple Health

If you allow it, CityRun saves each finished run to Apple Health as a workout (including its route), so it appears in Health and in other apps you have connected such as Strava. If you allow it, CityRun also reads your average heart rate for the period of the run to show on your run summary.

This exchange is entirely between CityRun and Apple Health on your device. Health data is never sent to us. You can revoke either permission at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health, and CityRun will keep working without it.

Camera and Motion

The camera is used only when you choose to take a selfie at a landmark. Motion data (step cadence and air pressure) is used only to smooth the pace shown on screen where GPS accuracy is poor, such as narrow streets, and to measure how much you climb. Neither is transmitted.

Other Apps

Directions open Apple Maps, a club's page opens in your browser, and Add to calendar uses iOS's own calendar sheet; what happens there is covered by those apps.

Usage Data

To learn which features are worth improving, CityRun sends anonymous usage counts — for example "a run was finished on the Lake route, 5–6 km, 4 of 6 landmarks", or "turn directions were switched off". They never include your location, your route track, your name, your health data or your photos, and distances and times are rounded so that a single run cannot be picked out. Each count also carries basic technical details, such as app version and where it was installed from, iOS version, device model, screen size and orientation, language, region, time zone, the date, light or dark mode, and accessibility settings — plus a random code that changes each time you open the app or come back to it after a few minutes away. There is no lasting identifier, and counts are never linked to you or used to track you.

These counts are processed by TelemetryDeck GmbH in the EU on our behalf. Turn them off at any time in Settings › Usage data; nothing is sent from then on.

Maps

CityRun's maps are built into the app, so they are drawn entirely on your phone. Showing a map does not contact a map provider or any other server, and no location or usage data is sent anywhere to display one.

Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License.

Run Club and Café Updates

When club updates are switched on in a future version, CityRun will download the current list of run clubs and partner cafés from our website at most once a day, so a changed meeting time reaches you without an app update. The request carries nothing about you — no identifier, no location, no account. Our website is hosted by GitHub, which sees the network address a request comes from, as any web host does; we don't use it to identify anyone.

What We Do Not Do

We do not use advertising or tracking of any kind, and apart from the anonymous counts above, no analytics. We do not build a profile of you. We do not sell, rent or share your data, because we do not receive it. CityRun does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework because it does not track you across apps or websites.

Deleting Your Data

Delete an individual run from the Runs tab; its selfies are deleted with it. To remove everything at once, delete the app — it removes all of your CityRun data from this device — and, if iCloud backup was on, delete CityRun's iCloud data as described under ICLOUD BACKUP. Workouts you previously sent to Apple Health stay in Health, and are deleted from there.

Children

CityRun is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will appear here and the date above will change.
