Common Questions

Does it work without mobile data? Yes. City maps, routes and the landmark stories are all built into the app, so nothing needs to download before or during a run. If a map ever appears blank, that is a bug — please email me.

I don't hear the stories. A story starts when you reach a landmark on the route, so the app stays quiet until you are on it. For now the stories are read aloud in English by your phone's own voice. They play even with your phone on silent, so check the volume.

My run did not save. CityRun keeps a running backup, so if the app closed mid-run it will offer to recover the run the next time you open it.

How do I delete my data? Delete a run from the Runs tab. Deleting the app removes everything on your phone; if iCloud backup is on, also delete CityRun's copy in iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › CityRun.
